Workspace privacy

The deploying organization manages member emails, roles, uploaded images, captions, schedules, review comments and delivery history. It uses Cloudflare for identity, storage and hosting. Administrators can view workspace records. Designers can view their own posts and media.

Submitting a post for review keeps its image private. Authorized dispatch gives Buffer a delivery URL and the selected channel’s caption. Anyone who obtains that URL can retrieve the image while delivery is active and for a grace period after confirmed publication. The relevant social platform receives the published content. Original files may contain embedded metadata.

A tab may keep unsaved caption edits in session storage. Use Sign out on shared devices. No AI service receives workspace content. For access, retention, correction or deletion requests, contact your organization’s workspace administrator.
